# SELARA BAY — Music & Sound

Everything is synthesized in the browser with Web Audio (`game/js/audio/sfx.js`): no samples.

- **Stings** — mission passed (rising major arpeggio), mission failed, FLATLINED (falling minor line),
  CUFFED (two-tone police motif), Orchid tag chime.
- **Sound effects** — engine with RPM and simulated gear shifts, tyre screech, crashes scaled by impact, horn,
  pistol / SMG / shotgun shots, reloads, punches, explosions, two-tone siren with doppler, helicopter rotor,
  footsteps, pickup and cash chimes, UI clicks.
- **Ambience** — distant traffic, birds by day, crickets at night, rain, waves near the sea.

Planned radio stations (not yet implemented): Selara Nights FM (synthwave), Ombak Lo-Fi, Gamelan Wave.
